# The window's layout

One of the features [the GPUI app](../README.md) has so far.

Lays the window out as the Tauri app does, with Trellis itself ported to GPUI (`crates/trellis`): Project and the Agent over Papers on the left, the documents between, the PDF on the right, each a rounded card with a 40px strip of tabs. Tabs drag between panels with Trellis's drop slot (an edge splits, the middle adds a tab), a panel's last tab takes the panel with it and its neighbours take its room, dividers resize with Trellis's rubber band and spring, panels float, maximize, hide and close from their ⋯ menu or keys, and a narrow strip gives way as Lattice's patch plans it. Free navigation (pinch and ⌃-wheel zoom, ⇧-drag marquee) frames any part of the layout. Workspaces, the Writing and Reading presets, focus mode and each project's layout run on Lattice's own layout functions (`shell/trellis_layout`, ported from `src/trellis/trellis-layout.ts`).

## In the e2e scenario

The `dock` module runs the window's own steps on the Trellis workspace (`scenario/shell_steps.rs`): the PDF dragged by its tab with native mouse events to the documents' left edge (a new split) and into their middle (a tab, its own panel closing up), the focused panel maximized and restored, the Writing preset and the way back, a new workspace keeping its own arrangement (the dot until it is saved), Project closed from its tab and brought back beside the Agent from the Panels toggle, the PDF floated and docked, a native double click on the float's strip background over a docked panel's strip reaching only the float (its view focused, the panel beneath not maximized), the layout read back as a relaunch reads it, the command palette listing the commands, and ⌘W on the focused document closing its tab with the keyboard left in the window (the palette still opens, ⌘⇧T brings the tab back).
A native drag has a frame drawn before its press and after every event, since input lands on the last frame's hitboxes; a window the platform is not presenting (covered, or on a locked screen) draws none itself, so the scenario draws it, and the step's detail records how many the window drew.
The `narrow_window` module: a narrow window (`scenario/e2e/narrow_window.rs`): at the CI runner's 1015 px and at the window's least, in light and dark and in English and Simplified Chinese with a hidden panel's chip in the titlebar, no part of the titlebar is drawn over another or past the window's edge and the build chip keeps to its strip; then, with four documents in a strip, the window narrows from 1015 px to its least and the build chip keeps its mark alone before the strip gives it up.
