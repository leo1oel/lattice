# Papers

One of the features [the GPUI app](../README.md) has so far.

Lists the papers the project's bibliography cites in the Papers panel, as the Tauri app does: one box that searches the library by its metadata at once and by the papers' saved text (their matching lines under each paper, each opening it at that line), and adds an arXiv id, DOI, URL or BibTeX entry it does not hold, with its full text when Add papers or Settings › Literature services says so (a known one says where it already is); a count of what is shown, filters (cited here, not cited yet, with full text, flagged), sorts and groups (year, venue, reading state); each paper's glyph, title, how often the manuscript cites it, byline, citation-health notice, a tip naming what a click does, and under the pointer its citation key to copy, its link and Remove (which asks first, and what to do with its citations); a click opens a saved paper's text or downloads it (the box showing the stage, with Cancel).
The box's down arrow comes into the list, whose arrows, Home and End walk the papers and their matching lines, Enter or Space doing what a click does; the up arrow from the first paper goes back to the box.
A paper the box just added arrives in the accent's wash, an empty library shows the card-index drawing, and the library's loading line fades in after a moment.
An import saves the citation alone unless Add papers' "Also save full text (Markdown)" is on; a paper already cited offers Refresh entry, which checks its entry against its sources and writes what they say unless that would replace a value the writer stated; and an import that finds the paper tools missing offers their install.
The palette lists Add papers, Discover literature and Check references under Research.
A maximised panel lays the papers out as a table and shows the selected paper in an inspector: its uses in the manuscript, abstract and record.
A paper's row drags out as the row itself, translucent under the pointer, and cites the paper where it lands.
Over a LaTeX source the drop caret goes after the word under the pointer, where `~\citep{key}` goes in, or at a key boundary of a citation command there, whose key list the key joins once; over a Markdown source it goes in at the pointer as an `@key` link to the paper's reading file; over a Markdown file's visual editor it is that editor's `@key` citation (see [Markdown files](markdown.md)).

Adds papers in bulk (Add papers: pasted ids, DOIs, URLs, titles or BibTeX, each looked up and added or reported, its full text saved too only with Also save full text (Markdown) ticked, off by default and shared with Settings › Literature services; a reference entered by hand; or papers ticked in the Zotero library's folders, each bringing its annotations, which go to the paper's note, and its PDF when Settings › Literature services saves full text; or a linked library, a reference manager's .bib whose new papers, updates, changes made here too and dropped papers are laid out to tick before any reach the project's .bib), discovers literature in a drawer searching OpenAlex and alphaXiv (a result added to Papers or cited through Add papers), checks the bibliography's references against their published records (Check references: findings grouped To fix, To review, Incomplete, Not checked, Kept and Verified, applied one by one or all at once, the report kept between runs; see below), and shows a `.bib` file as Edit, Split or Preview: its references as a searchable list (filtered by use, sorted by file order, year, author or title) beside the reference in front as a form whose every field edits the file in place, undoable from the source's history, with Add reference looking a citation up or taking it by hand.
Add reference lays its fields out as the Tauri app's form does, each label at its field's start: the records a lookup could not tell apart (each with its venue, whether it is the published version or the preprint, and how it was matched, with Keep my fields once the fields show), how the record it used was matched and that it was edited since, a year stepper, the BibTeX it will write a click away, and from Papers the choice to cite the entry at the caret once it is saved.
Check references reads as the Tauri app's sheet does: its filters are sliding tabs, each with its findings' status mark and count, the chosen one on a plate that slides to a newly chosen tab.
At 800px and wider its list stands beside an "Open finding" pane as a review queue, which opens on the first finding to act on; narrower, a finding unfolds under its row, whose folded Apply stays while the sheet is 520px or wider.
A fix shows the reference as it prints with its edits struck and inserted in place, each tagged with the fields it changes, and "Also changes" for the fields print does not show (a DOI, the entry type); then Apply, Skip and an "N of M" stepper, each moving on to the next finding.
An inline `\bibitem` is never rewritten: Go to it, or Copy corrected `\bibitem`, which changes only the words the item states when it states them all and writes the item afresh from the verified record otherwise.
"Fields and BibTeX" folds each field's value struck beside what it becomes, the proposed record and the entry as written.
An unverified candidate is compared field by field, Yours over "Not verified against" its source, only the differing words marked, with the conflicting fields and the candidate's BibTeX folded under Keep mine.
The queue's keys are the Tauri app's, bound in the sheet only: J and K walk the list, A or ⌘↩ applies the open fix and moves on, S skips to the next finding; the shortcut sheet lists them under Check references.

Reads a saved paper in its own tab, as the Tauri app does (`paper-reader.tsx`): its full text or its overview is the bundle's `paper.md` or `blog.md` in the visual editor's reading form (KaTeX, figures, the heading rail), the full text under its masthead of title and authors, editable in Edit, Split or Preview beside a Blog / Paper switch.
The strip above names the paper (its title and authors only while the masthead is off screen, and its source, which opens the paper on the web), folds to icons when narrow, and shows the original PDF in the text's place: the bundle's own, else arXiv's, alphaXiv's or a cited `.pdf`, fetched once.
A source link into the paper's PDF opens it there at its page; a paper opens again in the view it was left in, and on its PDF when it was read that way; a search hit or a link to a paper's text opens it in its reader at the line.

The Bibliography layout preset: the Papers library beside the bibliography, the `.bib` in front.

## In the e2e scenario

The Papers panel (`scenario/e2e/papers/`), on the project's own bibliography.
It appends an entry to `references.bib` and writes a saved paper's bundle to `.research/papers/1706.03762`, as an import would, and puts both back as they were before the next step.
It checks the listing with each paper's citation count, a search through the saved text and the metadata, the Cited filter, the list's keyboard from the box and back (`scenario/e2e/papers/library.rs`), the palette's Research rows with Add papers run from them, the full-text setting an import follows, Add reference entered by hand (its year stepper, its BibTeX preview and its cite-at-caret choice), an import asked to cite writing at the caret and ticking Get started's Cite step, Refresh entry leaving another project's bibliography alone, a click opening the paper in its reader with its row marked, Add papers reading a pasted entry and an id the library holds, an added paper bringing only its citation while Also save full text is off, its Zotero browser on a HOME with no library, and Check references reading every reference without checking any, then its review queue (`scenario/e2e/papers/audit.rs`), each reference given the finding a check would have: the queue opening beside the first fix, J, K and S walking it, A applying the open fix into the `.bib` and moving on, and the shortcut sheet's Check references keys; with `LATTICE_E2E_SHOTS=DIR` it saves the queue on a fix and on a candidate's comparison, light and dark, English and Chinese.
Then the paper's reader (`scenario/e2e/papers/reader.rs`), its bundle given an overview and an original PDF: from the page's own DOM, the full text in the visual editor's reading form under its masthead with its formula drawn by KaTeX, the strip leaving out the title and authors while that masthead is on screen; Blog showing the overview without a masthead, and the strip still leaving them out back on the full text's top; the original PDF in the text's place with the text's page put away, and the way back.
With `LATTICE_E2E_SHOTS=DIR` it saves the reader and its PDF in light and dark.
Then a citation key's card in `main.tex` (`scenario/e2e/papers/cards.rs`): it lists the key's uses here, each in its section with the page its note names; Why cited from the card files a reason with its role and note, which the card's row then shows, while the card stays away; a right-click on the key opens Why cited too; and a reason whose cite is deleted joins the Annotations panel's notes and the titlebar's count.
The file and the annotations are put back afterwards, and with `LATTICE_E2E_SHOTS=DIR` it saves the card and Why cited in light and dark, English and Chinese.

A paper dragged from the list (`scenario/e2e/paper_drag.rs`), on its own copy of that entry and bundle: a drop into a LaTeX source after the word under the pointer, with its caret drawn there first, and onto a `\citep`'s key, which the key joins; a drop into a Markdown source as an `@key` link; and a drop onto the file's visual editor, whose drop cursor shows while the paper is held over it, as an `@key` citation at the pointer, then that editor's `@` menu listing the paper.
With `LATTICE_E2E_SHOTS=DIR` it saves each drag held, in light and dark.

Then the `.bib` file's visual form (`scenario/e2e/bib.rs`): Preview lists the references and brings one to the front with its fields, a field typed in the form lands in the source buffer and ⌘Z steps it back, a field typed back to its first value is written too, a box left as another reference comes to the front writes only to the reference it was typed for, a box left with nothing typed shows the text the source changed it to, a reference deleted and brought back with ⌘Z gains no second copy from the notice's Undo, a reference renamed after a delete before it was undone stays in front under its new key, and Split shows the source beside the list.
Those edits stay in the editor's buffer of `references.bib` and are stepped back there.
