# Boards, spreadsheets and decks

One of the features [the GPUI app](../README.md) has so far.

Opens boards (`.excalidraw`; a `.tldr` opens as the `.excalidraw` converted beside it), spreadsheets (`.lattice-sheet`) and Open Slide decks (`slides/<deck>/index.tsx`) alone in its document's panel: a board in the native whiteboard (`gpui/crates/lattice-board`, quiet.md's Rust port of Excalidraw, restyled as the Tauri app's Excalidraw is, with Find on canvas and Export image), a sheet and a deck in the Tauri app's own editors, Univer and the Open Slide workspace, each a webview island (no status bar, no strip tools, as the Tauri canvas shows them), in the Tauri app's theme and language. Their text is the panel's: saved after a pause, on leaving and before the tab closes, merged with an outside edit, and an edit on disk merged into the open board or sheet. A sheet exports to Excel through the save panel; a deck's saves in Open Slide are written to the project, merged as the Tauri app merges them, when the Open Slide runtime is staged beside the app (`src-tauri/presentation-runtime` and the Node in `src-tauri/synara-runtime/bin`, or `LATTICE_GPUI_RESOURCES`); without it the deck says Open Slide could not start. Open Slide's Ask AI, Apply comments and Draft a deck show the Agent and hand its composer the request, as the Tauri app does.
The deck's selected slide and element are the Agent's presentation context, a deck with an inspector edit not yet committed is held from Overleaf until it lands, and a deck on a linked project whose Overleaf role cannot write is shown read-only.
When another project's window holds the Open Slide runtime, Switch to that window brings it forward.
