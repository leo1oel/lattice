# The PDF preview

One of the features [the GPUI app](../README.md) has so far.

Shows the PDF on the right, rendered natively by PDFium, in the Tauri app's viewer: page navigation (⌘↑ / ⌘↓ to the first and last page) and a typed page of digits only, Back and Forward over link jumps, the outline, Find in PDF (Match case, Whole word, the match counter), zoom steps, a typed zoom that Escape keeps, the wheel over the zoom value, ⌘-wheel / Ctrl-wheel and pinch around the pointer, Fit width and Fit height, dark pages and Save PDF as…, which shows that it is saving and takes one save at a time; a toolbar 640px or narrower folds the secondary controls into a ⋯ menu, as the Tauri app's does. A drag selects text and ⌘C / Ctrl+C (or ⌘X / Ctrl+X) copies it; a link jumps (Back returns) or opens in the browser. A rebuild redraws only the pages it changed. The live PDF can move into a window of its own (from its toolbar or the palette), giving its place in the workspace to the documents until it comes back; the window opens again, behind the workspace, when the project opens next; each other root document built keeps its last PDF in a tab of its own; a PDF of the project opens from the tree in a tab among the documents, follows its file on disk, and says "This PDF was removed from the project." while the file is gone, keeping its pages until the file returns; only a PDF a build makes suggests building again when it cannot load.
A first load shows a skeleton of the page where the page will be, and a refresh of the PDF on screen says "Updating…" over it, each only once the load outlasts a glance; with no PDF yet, Build project shows its keys (⌘S) and, while it builds, the infinity loader.
A middle press on the pages, off a link, scrolls them towards the pointer at a speed that follows its distance from the press (still near it), until a press, the wheel, Escape, the window going to the background or a new load of the PDF stops it.
Resting the pointer on a citation link (hyperref's `cite.KEY`, biblatex's `cite.N@KEY`) opens the entry's card above it (below it when there is no room) after 250 ms: the key, the title, the authors, the venue and year; the title opens the paper in its reader when Papers has its text or overview, fetches it first when Papers can (as a Papers row does, falling back to its source on the web when the download fails), else opens its source on the web (the entry's address, its DOI or arXiv), and the card closes 180 ms after the pointer leaves the link and the card, or at once on a click, a scroll or zoom, Escape or a new load of the PDF.
A root document's tab never spins its locate button for the live PDF's jump.
In a project using chemnum, resting the pointer on a compound's number (one the last build's `.aux` gave a compound) traces it back by reverse SyncTeX to its source line and shows the line's keys for that number as `\cmpd{key}` (`\rcmpd{…}` for a drawing's mark) with "Click to copy the LaTeX command"; a key on the card, or a plain click on the number when it names one key, copies the command, and the card closes 300 ms after the pointer leaves the number, the card and the 16px around them, or at once on a click elsewhere, a scroll or zoom, Escape or a new load of the PDF; citation numbers, references and years on the same line show none.
The project's open annotations are drawn on the pages: a note made on the PDF on the page it was made on, and one made in a .tex file over the words it quotes where SyncTeX puts its line in the last build (the line's whole width when its words don't read on the page), as an amber highlight with its numbered pin in the margin beside the column (blue while the agent's proposal waits, violet while its question does).
Resting the pointer on a highlight or its pin lights it, a click opens its comment card with Resolve and Reply, and Escape or a click elsewhere closes the card; each note is placed again off the main thread whenever the PDF is read anew, keeping its last place until then.
An image of the project (PNG, JPEG, WebP, SVG) opens from the tree in the Tauri app's image preview, in a tab of its own: fitted on its checkerboard stage at 100%, zoomed from 30% to 500% by its controls (Zoom out, a typed or wheeled percentage, Zoom in), ⌘-wheel / Ctrl-wheel and pinch, its zoom and scroll kept for the session; any other file lattice-core reads as binary (EPS, GIF, a font, an archive) says "This format cannot be rendered in the preview", as the Tauri app's does.
It jumps between the PDF and the source with SyncTeX: ⌘⇧J / Ctrl+Shift+J (or the PDF toolbar's locate button, which spins while the jump is located and is off while the caret is outside a .tex file) marks the caret's line in the PDF, a double-click in the PDF opens its source line with the caret on the clicked word (of overlapping jumps only the newest lands), a ⌘-double-click / Ctrl-double-click on a figure opens the drawing it was made from (the entry .tex's `% editable-figure source:` line, see [editable figures](../../docs/editable-figures.md)), asking first for an SVG, and off a figure is the ordinary jump, and an Outline row opens its heading and marks it in the PDF; the Outline marks the section the caret of the editor in front is in.

## In the e2e scenario

Its PDF steps write and build a second root document of twelve pages (`scenario/e2e/pdf/`): its PDF becomes the live one and main.tex keeps a tab of its own; then the outline (its heading lines kept past a comment block of wide characters), page navigation and a typed page, zoom and both fits, ⌘F / Ctrl+F with the query typed and Enter, a section link clicked with the native mouse and undone with Back (and links to a file or an app's own scheme left unfollowed), a selection dragged with the native mouse and copied with ⌘C / Ctrl+C, dark pages, a rebuild that changes one page and redraws only that one, the page window letting go of far pages, and the separate window, opened again when its project is shown again and taking the PDF out of a panel it shares with Project, which keeps its width, until Return puts it back, and main.tex's tab asking for a build that Stop then drops, so the next build compiles the document in front; building main.tex again trades the two tabs back. Then a second main window opens on the project, and its PDF must load and draw at the window's scale factor, through the same process-wide PDFium worker.

The `pdf_sync_polish` module (`scenario/e2e/pdf_sync_polish.rs`): a double-click on a word PDFium reads in the PDF landing the caret on that word of the source line, the palette wording the PDF's rows as the Tauri table does, a ⌘-double-click off a figure being the ordinary jump, only the newer of it and a plain double-click landing, and a PDF of the project open in a tab saying it was removed and taking the file back when it returns.
It goes on to the toolbar's states (`scenario/e2e/pdf_sync_states.rs`): Reveal cursor in PDF off and spinning while a jump is located, in the panel and in the PDF's own window, and off with the caret in a .bib file; focus mode giving the PDF up to its own window and showing it again on Return; the wheel over the zoom value stepping the zoom; a project PDF that fails to load without the build hint; and a ⌘-double-click on a figure drawn in an SVG asking before it opens, dismissed unanswered.
The `pdf_citations` module (`scenario/e2e/pdf_citations.rs`) builds a document with hyperref citing the fixture's `knuth1984`, rests the pointer on the citation link and checks that the card opens only after its delay and closes once the pointer leaves, then that it closes when the pointer leaves the pages straight from the link and on Escape with the focus off the PDF.
The `pdf_compounds` module (`scenario/e2e/pdf_compounds.rs`) builds a document numbering two chemnum compounds, rests the pointer on the printed `2` and checks the card names `\cmpd{alcohol}` and that a click on the number copies it; with main.tex's PDF then in a root tab, a jump to the live PDF spins only the live locate button.
The `pdf_notes` module (`scenario/e2e/pdf_notes.rs`) adds a note quoting words of chapters/intro.tex, checks that it is drawn as one band over those words (narrower than their line), that a click on it opens its card and Escape closes it, then deletes the note.
The `pdf_viewer_polish` module (`scenario/e2e/pdf_viewer_polish.rs`): a project PDF's first load showing the skeleton and a refresh of it showing "Updating…" instead, then a middle press on the zoomed live PDF scrolling the pages towards the pointer until a left press stops it, and Escape with the focus off the PDF and a press over the editor each stopping it too.
