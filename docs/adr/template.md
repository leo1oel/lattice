# NNNN. Short title in the imperative or as a noun phrase

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN | Superseded — <reason>
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who actually made the call

## Context

What situation forced a decision. Include the constraints that were real at the
time — versions, sizes, deadlines, what was and was not already shipped.

If you cite a measurement, say **what commit or version it was measured
against** and on what date. A number without a reference point is the single
most common way these documents go wrong.

## Decision

What was decided, stated plainly. One paragraph is usually enough.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what it obliges someone to do
later. Be specific about the obligations — "revisit when X" with no owner and no
trigger is how a temporary rule becomes a permanent one nobody remembers
agreeing to.

If the decision constrains how contributors work, **also** record it in
`CONTRIBUTING.md` or the relevant subsystem document. Nobody reads ADRs before
opening a pull request.

## Status changes

Append dated lines here when the decision is revisited, expires, or is
superseded. Do not silently edit the sections above — the value of an ADR is
that it shows what was believed at the time.
