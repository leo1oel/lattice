\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}
Attention replaced recurrence in sequence models \citep{vaswani2017attention}, and later work scaled it to pretraining \citep{devlin2019bert,brown2020language,missing2024key}.
Section~\ref{sec:method} describes the method, \autoref{sec:results} reports the results, and \cref{fig:case-failure,fig:cost} compare two cases.
The objective is \eqref{eq:loss}, and \Cref{tab:main} lists the scores; \ref{sec:missing} names nothing.

\section{Method}\label{sec:method}
We minimise the negative log-likelihood of the targets.
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L} = -\sum_i \log p(y_i \mid x_i)
  \label{eq:loss}
\end{equation}
